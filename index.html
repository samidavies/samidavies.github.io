About

I'm a researcher in theoretical CS. I primarily work on (offline and online) algorithms for combinatorial optimization problems. In particular, I tend to like scheduling and clustering problems. Much of my work the past several years has focused on models that go beyond worst-case analysis.

I was a research scientist in EECS at UC Berkeley for 3 years until fall 2026. Before coming to Berkeley, I was a post-doc in the theory group at Northwestern for 2 years. In June 2021, I graduated with my Ph.D. in math from the University of Washington, where I worked between the Department of Mathematics and the theory group in the Allen School. I was advised by Thomas Rothvoss.
